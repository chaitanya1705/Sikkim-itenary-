How high you'll go

Tap any point. Day 2 takes you from about 5,400 ft to 14,450 ft and back in one day, so pace yourself above the shaded line.

Go slow up high. Walk, don't run, at the high stops. Short visits are the plan anyway.
Hydrate and keep warm. Sip water through the climb. Wind at the pass makes it feel much colder.
Know the signs. Headache, dizziness or breathlessness that gets worse means head down and tell your driver.

Permits, sorted

Only 8 November needs real preparation. Your registered local agency or cab operator applies for the protected-area and Nathula permits, at least one day before.

Hand to the agency on 7 Nov

Nathula is open to Indian nationals only, and the current guidance lists Monday as the no-visit day. 8 Nov 2026 is a Sunday. Photography is prohibited at the pass itself, so follow what security personnel say.

Packing checklist

November means mild afternoons in Gangtok, possible snow at Nathula and near-freezing pre-dawn at Tiger Hill. Ticks are saved on this device.
